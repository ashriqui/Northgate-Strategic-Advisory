03

Delivery

Deliver

Everyone is right. Not everyone is aligned.

A company is one entity made up of several teams, each with its own job and its own goals. All of them are right. The work is keeping them pointed the same way as a program grows.

As project manager on the client's behalf, we coordinate production, suppliers and the end user, so what was agreed is what gets built, delivered and accepted.

  1. 1Coordinate. The client, production, suppliers and end user, on one schedule.
  2. 2Track. Milestones, risks and issues, with one point of contact.
  3. 3Resolve. Whatever is blocking the plan, early.
