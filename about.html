Who We Are

Based in Toronto.
Working across North America.

Northgate Strategic Advisory is a Toronto-based advisory and project management firm. We began as an advisory for small businesses entering defence, and have grown into a full-service partner with experience in both defence and consumer packaged goods.

Our background spans Canadian defence manufacturing, institutional sales, government procurement and consumer packaged goods. We have built revenue programs from a standing start, and we bring that execution-first approach to every engagement. We work with a small number of clients at any one time.

Our experience includes

  • Department of National Defence Canada
  • Global Affairs Canada
  • United States Navy
  • Department of Homeland Security
  • PepsiCo Beverages Canada
  • Frito-Lay Canada
